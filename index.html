Reading the first result

A failed candidate can tell you where to look.

The balanced Triangle rules admit 91 of 462 sampled locations. The No global fit preset admits none. At the strongest remaining sample, the canal margin is −11 units: that location fails one of the five rules.

The preset changes several buffers. That candidate diagnosis does not attribute the whole loss of candidates to the canal alone. Zero passing grid points also does not establish that every point between samples is impossible.

What the result leaves open

A compatible sample is a candidate under supplied rules. It supplies neither a survey nor an engineering assessment. The finite checks do not establish the stronger sheaf condition of unique gluing.

Read the Triangle’s construction and limits

Working principles across the later essays
  • Local first. Keep each team’s own useful view before attempting composition.
  • Overlaps explicit. Say which facts must agree across which interfaces.
  • Failure is evidence. A mismatch can locate an obstruction rather than merely create a red light.
  • Checker, not oracle. Language models may propose structure; declared rules judge compatibility.
  • Plural essays. Hold concrete attempts apart until integration is earned.
