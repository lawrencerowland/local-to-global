Local to Global

Toy scenario

Fit a control centre onto the Tangled Triangle.

A fictional triangular site is crossed by a canal, road, railway viaduct, overhead power line and mine passage. Each team sees different constraints. Early sketches ask how AI interprets that brief; later models ask whether the declared local constraints leave any compatible overall solution.

Can locally sensible proposals agree at their shared boundaries?

Two connected lines of enquiry: how AI interprets a project's interfaces and architecture, and when local accounts can compose into a coherent global view. The Tangled Triangle connects them while keeping their questions distinct.

Can scope, state, schedule or risk be represented so that a project-wide view is composed from local data without erasing the disagreements at its boundaries?The main foray question
