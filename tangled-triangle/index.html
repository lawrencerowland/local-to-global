Tangled Triangle · two lines of enquiry

One difficult site.
Two different questions.

The early work asks how well AI can make sense of interfaces, topology and system architecture. The later work reuses the same scenario to ask whether declared local constraints can compose into a compatible global account.

1 · Early enquiry

AI interpretation of interfaces, topology and system architecture

A regional signalling control centre occupies a fictional triangular site, with a canal, road, railway viaduct, overhead power line and mine passage. These six variants expose different interpretations of that brief so their diagrams, assumptions, omissions and interface questions can be inspected side by side. They are separate explorations, not a ranked sequence or a single settled design.

Evidence boundary: this collection is not an empirical AI benchmark: it supplies no controlled model comparison, scored reference interpretation or repeatability study. Its drawings and architecture alternatives do not establish engineering feasibility or safety.
